This course covers the design and implementation of hardware and software interfaces connecting computer processing units with external peripheral devices, sensors, actuators, and communication buses.

### Course Description
This course focuses on computer interface components, programmable interfacing integrated circuits, sensing mechanisms, and hardware communication protocols. Students work with microcontroller and microprocessor development platforms (including AVR/Arduino and STM32 ARM Cortex architectures) and the Programmable Peripheral Interface (PPI - 82C55). Topics include electrical signal conditioning, high-power interface drivers, sensor and actuator integration, display modules (LED, LCD, seven-segment), synchronous and asynchronous communication protocols (I2C, SPI, UART), interrupt systems, ADC/DAC conversions, and real-world embedded system project design.

### Course Objectives
- Introduce interfacing components, electrical constraints, bus standards, and signal characteristics.
- Explain diverse sensor types, analog/digital sensing devices, and their physical calibration.
- Master General Purpose Input/Output (GPIO) programming using MCU development platforms and the Programmable Peripheral Interface (PPI) – 82C55.
- Study hardware communication protocols including UART, I2C, and SPI for inter-device messaging.
- Train students in driving output modules such as alphanumeric LCDs, OLED displays, relays, motors, and seven-segment units.
- Guide student teams in prototyping comprehensive embedded interfacing projects.

### Course Topics & Outline
- **Interfacing Fundamentals:** Microprocessor and microcontroller bus architectures, memory and I/O mapping, buffering, and address decoding.
- **Programmable Peripheral Interface (PPI – 82C55):** Internal architecture, control word formatting, Bit Set/Reset (BSR) mode, and I/O operating modes (Mode 0, 1, 2).
- **Microcontroller Platforms & GPIO:** Architecture, registers, and programming with AVR/Arduino and STM32 ARM Cortex platforms.
- **Sensor Integration:** Interfacing analog and digital sensors (temperature, ultrasonic distance sensors, PIR motion detectors, light sensors).
- **Actuators & Power Drivers:** Transistor switches, relays, optocouplers, H-bridges, DC motor speed control (PWM), and servo motor positioning.
- **Serial Communication Standards:** Asynchronous communication (UART/USART, RS-232/TTL levels), and synchronous serial buses (SPI, I2C).
- **Analog-to-Digital (ADC) & Digital-to-Analog (DAC):** Successive approximation ADC, resolution, sampling rates, DAC architectures, and signal generation.
- **Display Interfacing:** Driving 7-segment displays (multiplexing), 16x2 HD44780 alphanumeric LCDs, and OLED displays.
- **Interrupts & Timers:** Hardware interrupt vectors, external interrupt handling, timer/counter configuration, and real-time event management.
- **Project Prototyping:** Complete embedded system design from circuit schematics to firmware development.
