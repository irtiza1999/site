### Course Description
This course provides a comprehensive introduction to robotics, exploring mechanical structures, kinematics, sensor perception, actuator systems, feedback control, and autonomous decision-making. Students learn the mathematical formulation of spatial descriptions and homogeneous coordinate transformations, manipulator forward and inverse kinematics using Denavit-Hartenberg (D-H) parameters, differential kinematics, and trajectory planning. The course also addresses wheeled mobile robot kinematics, motor drive electronics, multi-sensor perception (ultrasonic, LiDAR, IMU, cameras), computer vision algorithms for robotic navigation, and hardware system integration.

### Course Objectives
- Introduce core robotics laws, system architectures, coordinate frames, and control hierarchies.
- Develop rigorous mathematical formulations for spatial representations, homogeneous transformation matrices, and forward/inverse kinematics.
- Study actuation technologies, motor control circuits (H-bridges, PWM), and differential drive locomotion.
- Cover sensor perception, sensor fusion, computer vision algorithms, and environmental feature extraction.
- Guide student teams through the complete design, construction, and programming of an autonomous robotic system.

### Course Topics & Outline
- **Introduction to Robotics:** Robotic classification, autonomy levels, work envelopes, and degree of freedom (DOF) analysis.
- **Spatial Representations & Transformations:** Translation, rotation matrices, Euler angles, and 4x4 homogeneous transformation matrices.
- **Manipulator Kinematics:** Forward kinematics using Denavit-Hartenberg (D-H) conventions, link parameters, and inverse kinematics solutions.
- **Mobile Robotics & Locomotion:** Differential drive kinematics, non-holonomic constraints, wheel odometry, and dead reckoning.
- **Actuators & Motor Control:** DC motors, stepper motors, servo motors, motor drivers, encoders, and PWM control.
- **Robotic Sensing & Perception:** Encoders, ultrasonic rangers, infrared sensors, Inertial Measurement Units (IMUs), and LiDAR.
- **Robot Vision:** Camera modeling, perspective projection, color segmentation, contour detection, and visual tracking.
- **Control Systems:** Open-loop vs. closed-loop control, Proportional-Integral-Derivative (PID) controllers, and error compensation.
- **Path Planning & Navigation:** Bug algorithms, potential fields, grid-based path planning (A* search), and obstacle avoidance.
- **System Integration:** End-to-end hardware prototyping and software control for autonomous robotic tasks.
